Daily routine (about 2–3 hours)

  1. Check Today (5 min). Open a pick and click Brief. It collects the real keyword, related searches, questions, sources and internal links.
  2. Write the fast article (60–90 min). 800–1,200 words. Answer the main query in the first 2 sentences. Add an original preview image or a free download, a short FAQ, and links to 3–5 of your posts.
  3. Publish and get indexed (5 min). Search Console → URL Inspection → Request indexing. Also submit through IndexNow (Rank Math Instant Indexing), which covers Bing and ChatGPT search.
  4. Distribute (10 min). Pin 2 images on Pinterest. Share on X or Facebook groups. Answer the matching Reddit question if one exists (only where it helps, with no link spam).
  5. Write the planned article (60–90 min). Use the Seasonal or Questions tab. Aim for 1,200–2,000 words with a comparison table, licence notes and an FAQ.
  6. Weekly (Friday, 30 min). In Search Console, find posts at positions 8–20 and improve them: add questions, update the year, add internal links. Then run the Site audit tab.

Playbook: SEO, AEO, GEO and authority (DR)

What to do on designerslab.io, in priority order. Run the Site audit tab to see which items your pages already pass.

1. Get every new article indexed within hours
  • After publishing, use Search Console → URL Inspection → Request indexing.
  • Install Rank Math → Instant Indexing (IndexNow). It pings Bing, Yandex and Seznam the moment you publish. ChatGPT search and Copilot use Bing's index.
  • Add the site to Bing Webmaster Tools (you can import it from Search Console in one click) and submit sitemap_index.xml.
  • Link the new post from the homepage "Latest" block and from 2–3 older, already-indexed posts. Google finds new URLs through internal links fastest.
  • Apply for Google News in Publisher Center, and enable the News sitemap if you publish trend articles often.
2. On-page SEO for each article
  • Put the exact keyword in the title (≤60 chars), URL slug, H1 and first 100 words. Write a 140–160 character meta description.
  • Use one H1. Base your H2s on Google's related queries and autocomplete (the Brief button lists them).
  • Add original images (previews of the font or mockup) as WebP, with descriptive alt text and file names. Google Images and Pinterest bring a lot of design traffic.
  • Add 3–5 internal links to related posts and to the matching resource category. Add 1–2 external links to the official source (foundry, brand, news).
  • For trend posts, update the article when news changes and keep the date honest. Keep "max-image-preview:large" enabled for Google Discover.
3. AEO: win featured snippets, People Also Ask and voice answers
  • Phrase 2–4 H2s exactly like real questions ("What font is the Ohio State logo?").
  • Answer directly in the first 40–60 words under each question, then expand.
  • End with an FAQ block (Rank Math FAQ block, which adds FAQPage schema) of 3–5 questions from the Questions tab.
  • Use comparison tables (font, style, licence, download) and numbered steps for how-tos (HowTo-style lists).
4. GEO: get cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews

  • Keep /llms.txt current. List your best guides and resource categories with a one-line description each.
  • Write clear, self-contained facts that AI can quote, e.g. "The Netflix logo uses Netflix Sans, a custom typeface made by Dalton Maag in 2018."
  • Add original data: "Most downloaded mockups this month", "Top 20 fonts searched in 2026" (use this tool's data). AI engines and journalists cite statistics.
  • Show author expertise (bio, portfolio, Person schema) and keep dateModified current. Mention the brand the same way everywhere (Designers Lab / designerslab.io).
5. Raise Domain Rating (backlinks) safely
  • Use your free resources as link bait. On every download page, add "Free for commercial use, credit appreciated: Designers Lab" with a copyable HTML credit link.
  • Resource-page outreach: search "free mockups" inurl:resources, "design resources" "useful links" and email the page owners about your collection.
  • Digital PR with data: publish a monthly "Design search trends" report from this tool and pitch it to design blogs and newsletters.
  • Journalist requests: answer design and branding questions on Qwoted, Featured.com and SourceBottle. Quotes earn links from high-DR sites.
  • Profiles and communities: Behance, Dribbble, Pinterest, Product Hunt (launch the AI studio or the color generator), and design directories. Reddit and Quora answers that solve the problem.
  • Creators you feature: tell the foundries and mockup creators (Pixelbuddha, Mockupbee…) when you feature them. Many share or link back.
  • Guest posts on design and marketing blogs (2 per month), plus broken-link building for dead "free fonts" pages. Avoid paid link farms and PBNs, which can get the site penalised.
6. Track what works
  • Search Console → Performance → filter by page. A trend article should get impressions within 24–48 hours. If it doesn't, check indexing.
  • Keep a simple log: date, keyword, source (trend, angle, seasonal or question), indexed (yes/no), clicks after 7 and 30 days. Do more of what wins.
  • Each month, refresh the top 10 posts (year, new items, FAQ) and re-request indexing.
